Good questions

What to know before you start.

?

What is AI visibility?

It is how easily AI-powered discovery tools can understand and surface your business when someone asks for a relevant service. The answers can vary by platform, location, wording and time.

?

Can you guarantee ChatGPT or Google will recommend me?

No. These systems control their own results, and no legitimate provider can guarantee a specific recommendation. We focus on improving the quality and clarity of your public business information and measuring what can be observed.

?

Do I need a website?

A website can help, but it is not the only signal. Accurate business profiles, consistent details, trustworthy reviews and useful public information can also matter. Your best starting point depends on your business.

?

Is this a live audit or a managed service?

This starter website provides an example checklist in your browser. A real audit, monitoring dashboard, expert review, lead capture and ongoing managed service require a connected backend and a defined delivery process.
